# LockedIn

### What is LockedIn

LockedIn is a dynamic social media platform designed to bring people together through niche communities with shared goals. Whether it’s studying, working out, or pursuing hobbies, users can create or join communities where accountability thrives. Set a specific time for your activity, receive reminders, and post proof of participation with a picture. Earn consistency and progress ratings based on your engagement and track your journey in each community. LockedIn makes achieving goals more social, interactive, and rewarding—one post at a time!
